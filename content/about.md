I'm Daan Schutte, a former military pilot and flight instructor moving into
cybersecurity. I spent 20 years in military aviation, first with the South
African Air Force and later instructing in Qatar, and logged more than 2,800
flight hours along the way. In between, I worked as a backend software engineer
at Picnic in the Netherlands, building Spring Boot microservices.

As an Aviation Safety Officer at two squadrons I ran occurrence reporting and
analysis and monthly safety training. That experience is a large part of why
I'm drawn to security, and where aviation safety and cybersecurity overlap is
part of what I write about on this blog.

To build practical, foundational experience, I completed TryHackMe's SAL1
certification and worked hands-on with alert triage and log analysis in
Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk. SOC analysis is my preferred entry point. From
there, I'm drawn to the technical problem solving of L2/L3 analysis and
incident response, and Cyber Threat Intelligence is a field I'm keen to
explore.

My certification route:

- **TryHackMe SAL1:** completed November 2026
- **Microsoft SC-200:** next, for Sentinel and Defender depth
- **CompTIA Security+:** after SC-200

I'm based in The Hague, the Netherlands.
